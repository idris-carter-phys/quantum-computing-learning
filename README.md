# Quantum Computing Learning

Exercises, circuits, and notes built while working through quantum computing fundamentals as part of my path toward quantum computing research.

## About

I'm Idris Carter, an Engineering Physics undergraduate at Tulane University with a minor in AI, pursuing quantum computing research. This repository documents my self-directed study of quantum information science using IBM's Qiskit learning platform.

## Topics Covered

- Qubit fundamentals and quantum gates
- Quantum circuits and measurement
- Bell states and entanglement
- Quantum algorithms (Deutsch-Jozsa, Grover's — in progress)

## Tools

- Qiskit
- Python (NumPy, SciPy)
- IBM Quantum (cloud access for real hardware)
- Google Colab

## Goals

Building toward undergraduate research in quantum information and quantum machine learning, targeting summer REU programs and lab positions at Tulane's quantum physics department.
